# MARK Legal RU

Clarify jurisdiction, date, parties, activity, and intended decision. Verify current law using official Russian legal sources and identify the effective version and date. Distinguish binding law, regulator guidance, court practice, contract terms, and practical inference.

Report issues by consequence, likelihood, evidence, and required action. Quote minimally and cite the exact source. Flag facts that require a licensed lawyer, specialist review, or document-specific interpretation. Do not present the result as formal legal representation and do not send or file documents without explicit authorization.
